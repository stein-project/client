# Stein Client 0.2.3: new mods and test channel

The Stein Client now lists Keybind and Replay and is ready for test versions.

- **New mods**: Keybind (chat messages and commands on a key) and Replay (the replay of your death) join the mod list, on by default in a new install.
- **Test channel**: ready to receive test versions of Stein Loader and the mods before they go out to everyone. Only testers see it; nothing changes for players.
