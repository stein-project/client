# Stein Client 0.3.8: the graphics API chosen in game

Stein now lets you switch between Vulkan and OpenGL inside the game, in the video settings, and the launcher follows that choice.

- **Vulkan or OpenGL from the game**: what you pick in the game's video settings also holds the next times you open it from the launcher, and shows in its settings.
- **The automatic fallback stays**: graphics cards that cannot run Vulkan still open in OpenGL by themselves.
