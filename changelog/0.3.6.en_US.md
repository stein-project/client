# Stein Client 0.3.6: OpenGL by itself where Vulkan does not run

Where Vulkan does not run, the game now opens with OpenGL without you having to change anything.

- **Automatic switch to OpenGL**: when the game finds that the graphics card or its driver lacks what Stein's Vulkan needs, the launcher opens the game with OpenGL from then on and tells you why. After a driver update you can choose Vulkan again in Settings.
