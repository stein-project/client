# Stein Client 0.2.6: Stein GFX switch

Stein GFX got a switch in the Stein Client settings, for everyone.

- **Stein GFX in the settings**: on by default; off, the game opens with the usual renderer. Takes effect the next time the game opens.
- **Graphics card without OpenGL 4.1**: the Stein Client turns the switch off by itself when the game cannot open with Stein GFX; turning it on again makes it try once more.
