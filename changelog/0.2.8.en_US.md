# Stein Client 0.2.8: third-party mods

Mods from other authors can now be installed and updated by the Stein Client, just like the official ones.

- **Third-party mods**: in Mods, add a source by pasting the link of a mod's index (steinmods.json) or the index itself. The Stein Client shows who publishes it and where it comes from, warns that third-party mods are not reviewed by Stein, and from then on downloads and updates those mods every time you click Play. Removing a source keeps its mods in the folder; they just stop updating.
- **Combat and Mouse are now third-party mods**: both became open source and are published by their author, X4vieer, from repositories of their own. If you have them, nothing changes: the Stein Client added their new source on its own and keeps them updated. They are no longer installed by default.
- **Official mods from their own indexes**: each official mod now publishes its own index with every release, and the Stein Client reads them directly. Every file is still checked (sha256 and the mod inside it) before it goes into the mods folder.
