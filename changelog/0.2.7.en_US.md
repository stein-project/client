# Stein Client 0.2.7: a new home

The Stein Client now gets its updates and downloads from the Stein-Project organization on GitHub.

- **Updates from Stein-Project**: new versions of the Stein Client come from github.com/Stein-Project/stein-client, and Stein Loader, its installer and the mods from github.com/Stein-Project/stein-loader. Nothing changes in how you use it.
- **Stein Bridge and Stein Loader are open source**: the engine and the mod platform the Stein Client installs are now public, under the Mozilla Public License 2.0.
