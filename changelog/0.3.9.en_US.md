# Stein Client 0.3.9: test versions and a warning for incompatible mods

You can now get new Stein versions before everyone else, and the launcher warns you when a mod may not work with the installed Stein.

- **Test versions**: turn them on in Settings and the launcher installs the test versions of the Stein and of the mods as soon as they come out. Off, it goes back to the regular versions.
- **Incompatible mod warning**: before the game opens, the launcher checks every mod and warns you if one may fail with this Stein version. You decide whether to play anyway.
- **Novidades**: a page with what changed in each version of the Stein and of the mods, with a tab for the extras and other authors' mods.
- **Summary before playing**: under the Play button, the Stein version, Java, graphics and memory.
- **Accounts on their own page**: from the Accounts button at the bottom of the sidebar.
- **Removed mods leave the folder**: turning a mod off or removing its source takes its file out of the mods folder. Its settings are kept.
- **Plainer wording** across the launcher.
