# Stein Client 0.3.7: additions, and Keybind becomes an extra

Mods outside the defaults get a section of their own, and sources that change address sort themselves out.

- **Additions section**: under Mods, the mods for specific servers are kept apart from the default ones. They update like the others.
- **Keybind**: no longer comes by default on new installs. Whoever already has it keeps it, with the same keys.
- **Combat and Mouse**: they changed address, and the launcher fixes the source by itself. Mods, settings and keys stay the same.
- **No more banned mods**: the launcher no longer blocks mods by a list.
