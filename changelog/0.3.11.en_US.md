# Stein Client 0.3.11: Sprint turned on for whoever had Combat

Toggle sprint moved from Combat to its own mod, Sprint, which comes with the Stein Client: whoever already had Combat gets Sprint turned on by itself, with their old key.

- **Sprint for Combat players**: if Combat is installed, the launcher turns Sprint on once and says so. Sprint brings over the toggle sprint key and setting you had in Combat on its first start.
- **Your choice stays**: it happens only once. If you turn Sprint off afterwards, it stays off.
